# You let the smoke out

Back in August I [opened the bench for work](/post/2026-08-09-board-repair-open/) — a page on
this site, a mailto button, and a promise to be straight with people about what's fixable.
Since then the repair side has grown into something that deserves more than a section of my
personal site.

So it's a shop now: **[YOLO Electronics Repair](https://yolorepair.com/).** Board-level repair
out of West Sacramento. *You let the smoke out. We put it back in.*

## Why a separate name

Mostly because the people looking for a repair aren't looking for me. Somebody with a dead
laptop wants a repair shop — not a DJ's blog with a resume tab and a post about IRC bots.
Giving the work its own name and its own site means a customer lands somewhere that's only
about getting their thing fixed.

The name is Yolo County — West Sac sits right on the line — plus the obvious other reading,
which fits a business whose whole premise is taking a hot-air station to something
expensive. It's a little bit of a joke. The work isn't.

## What it fixes

The same work I was already doing, sharpened into four things:

- **Laptops** — logic-board diagnosis, dead charge circuits, shorted rails, liquid damage.
- **GPUs** — the no-power and no-display cards, diagnosed on a
  [rig built for exactly that](/post/2026-09-28-pcie-gpu-test-rig/).
- **DJ gear** — mixers and controllers. Faders, pots, and channels that cut out right
  before a gig.
- **Retro hardware** — recaps and restorations of the machines worth keeping alive.

Local drop-off in West Sacramento, mail-in from anywhere. **No-fix, no-fee on diagnosis**
still holds, and so does the list of things the shop won't take — that's on the
[Repair Services page](/page/electronics-repair/).

## What stays here

This site keeps the bench itself — [the gear](/page/electronics-bench/),
[the streams](/page/electronics-streaming/), and the software I write to run it, which is all
public now. [bench-suite](/post/2026-11-16-bench-suite/) puts the whole stack on one machine,
and its apt repo lives at apt.yolorepair.com, which is about as clear a sign as any that the
tooling and the shop grew up together.

The shop is where you send a board. This is where I write about what happened to it.

If you've got something that let the smoke out, [send it the details](/page/electronics-repair/).
