# A shelf, not a portfolio

The [Projects section](/page/projects/) has been quietly filling up, and I've reached the
point where I can't remember what's on there without looking. So here's the whole shelf in
one place, grouped by what it's actually for.

None of this is a product. It's the stuff I built because I wanted it to exist, and it's all
either open source or on its way there.

## Sound and light

The oldest thread, and the one that started everything else.

- **[Tech-Noid Systems](/page/tech-noid-systems/)** — the bass music and visual collective:
  streaming, live shows, and the tooling behind the feed.
- **[obs-radio-output](/page/obs-radio-output/)** — a native OBS plugin that streams straight
  to Icecast and SHOUTcast, no separate encoder in the chain. Public beta, Windows included
  now.
- **[Audiophore](/page/audiophore/)** — audio analysis → every light in the room. A
  low-latency bridge from Synesthesia to Hue, WLED, Art-Net, and lasers.

## The bench

This is where most of the recent energy went. Somewhere along the way the infrastructure
habit went physical.

- **[benchhud](/page/benchhud/)** — a heads-up display for the microsoldering bench. Scope
  feed, thermal camera registered on top of it so a component's heat bloom lands on the
  component, instrument telemetry, all in one window and composited straight into a stream.
- **[OpenBoardView](/page/openboardview/)** — not mine. Someone else's board viewer that I
  patched because board-to-schematic cross-probing silently did nothing on macOS. [The story
  of that one](/post/2026-07-29-openboardview-macos-pdf-bridge/) is a decent lesson in how a
  missing feature can look exactly like a broken one.

## Printed things

- **[3D Printer Models](/page/3d-printer-models/)** — a dozen models kept as OpenSCAD source,
  so the STL is an output you can regenerate rather than a mesh you're stuck with. Mostly
  bench organization, some VJ rig hardware.
- **[Clickfinity (OpenSCAD)](/page/clickfinity-openscad/)** — magnet-free Gridfinity
  baseplates. [Just went public](/post/2026-08-08-clickfinity-public/): it clicks, it tiles,
  and any grid size is two knobs.

## Tools

- **[claude-project-kit](/page/claude-project-kit/)** — scaffolding so an AI-assisted session
  starts already grounded instead of re-deriving the project every time.
- **[apptracker](/page/apptracker/)** — self-hosted job-hunt tracking that lives in your
  cluster instead of one browser's local storage.
- **[recipe-card-maker](/page/recipe-card-maker/)** — markdown recipes in, printable binder
  pages and 4×6 tin cards out.

## Chat

- **[annoybots](/page/annoybots/)** — the eggdrop and BMotion era rebuilt as one Go binary
  across IRC, Twitch, and Discord. The Markov brain still babbles, and it still occasionally
  says something better than anyone in the channel.

## And one that isn't software

- **[Emotional Support Pizza](/page/emotional-support-pizza/)** — artisan baked pizza, breads,
  and pastries. Every list needs one entry that breaks the pattern.
