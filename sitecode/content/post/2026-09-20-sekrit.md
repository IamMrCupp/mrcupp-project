# The thing I wasn't saying much about

The Projects page has had a vague "coming up" line on it for a couple of weeks. Here's
what it was.

[sekrit](/page/sekrit/) is an open, self-hostable event and ticketing platform for
independent promoters and underground scenes. It started from a specific irritation: every
platform in this space sells scarcity — invite trees, lotteries, locked lists — and then
keeps the two things a promoter actually needs. The attendee list you show a venue to
prove your draw. And your own money.

So the design is two rules and a feature:

- **No gatekeeping.** Anyone with the link can RSVP. Capacity is whatever the room holds,
  shown honestly.
- **Never in the flow of funds.** You attach your own Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or Stripe
  link and the platform renders the button. Money goes from the attendee to you. There is
  no platform account it passes through, on purpose — that's the line between a tool and a
  money transmitter.
- **You own your list.** CSV export, any time, of every contact you've collected. Not a
  retention hostage.

It's built for the NorCal bass scene first, because that's the one I'm in and the one
whose door already runs on "cash or Venmo @whoever." Generalizable after. FastAPI and
Postgres, Next.js up front, and it'll land on the same cluster as everything else here.

Pre-alpha, private repo, intended to be open source. The [project page](/page/sekrit/)
has the fuller pitch. It gets a public repo the moment there's something to put a QR code
on.
