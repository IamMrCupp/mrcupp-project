Somewhere along the way the infrastructure habit went physical. I've spent twenty-odd
years keeping systems alive at the software and network layer — lately I've been going a
lot deeper, down to the board itself. Microsoldering, component-level diagnosis, and the
vintage machines that got me into computers in the first place.

This is where that work lives — the bench, the tooling, and the streams.

**The repair side is a shop now: [YOLO Electronics Repair](https://yolorepair.com/).**
Laptops, GPUs, DJ gear, and retro hardware, out of West Sacramento, with local drop-off and
mail-in. If you've got a board that needs someone to actually find the failed part,
[that's the place to start](/page/electronics-repair/).

### Where to go from here
- [Repair Services](/page/electronics-repair/) — what the shop fixes, what it won't take,
  and how to send the details. Or go straight to [yolorepair.com](https://yolorepair.com/).
- [The Bench](/page/electronics-bench/) — the gear and tooling behind the work.
- [Streaming](/page/electronics-streaming/) — repair and build work, live.

The software that runs the bench is all public now:
[bench-suite](/page/bench-suite/) installs [benchhud](/page/benchhud/),
[benchhud-intake](/page/benchhud-intake/), [bench-parts](/page/bench-parts/), and
[bench-recon](/page/bench-recon/) in one go, and [burnwright](/page/burnwright/) handles the
chip programmer.
