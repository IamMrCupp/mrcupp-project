The plan is to put the bench on camera. Board repair is oddly watchable — a dead device
comes in, you find the one failed part under the scope, and it comes back to life. The
build projects too. It's the kind of work that's more fun to share than to do alone.

The streams will run on [benchhud](/page/benchhud/), the heads-up display I'm building for
the bench — scope feed, thermal overlay, and live instrument readings composited straight
into the stream, so you see what I'm seeing.

This is still spinning up. Channels get announced here and across [my
socials](/page/about/) once they're live — no dates to promise yet, but it's coming.
